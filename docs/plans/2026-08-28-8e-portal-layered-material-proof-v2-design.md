# Portal Layered Material Motion Proof v2 Implementation Plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Build a no-network, no-paid-call research composition that makes the confirmed Portal mother image feel alive through material-native layered motion while preserving geometry and loopability.

**Architecture:** Keep the mother image as an immutable base texture. Add only masked, feathered, low-frequency material passes for the Rift, road/reflection, crystal refraction, right field, depth bands, and foreground surface light. A deterministic phase clock drives every pass, with a gentle local central swell kept separate from the idle loop.

**Tech Stack:** Self-contained HTML/CSS/SVG/Canvas composition, local HyperFrames 0.8.14 renderer for research output, FFmpeg/ffprobe for encoded evidence, Python/Pillow/NumPy for metrics. No runtime dependency and no external network/model calls.
