## Adoption gates

Before a row moves from research/preview to shipped:

1. The consumer exists in a real product contract; a visual reference cannot
   create a Timeline, Training plan, rank, or evidence claim.
2. The source, license, and redistribution boundary are recorded. Prompt
   metadata alone is not a license.
3. The mechanism works at 1440, 1024, 390, and 320 pixels, has keyboard/focus
   semantics, and has a reduced-motion/static fallback.
4. The bundle and runtime cost are measured. CSS/SVG is preferred for simple
   geometry; an extra animation engine needs a reproducible Bad Case.
5. The visual can be removed without changing the data/control flow. The
   product must remain honest and usable with the row disabled.

## Current conclusion

The current journey adopts self-authored semantic DOM/SVG/CSS, one generated
background plate without text/UI/core, a CSS/SVG-only interactive core, and the
existing Motion runtime. The approved mother image and V2 keyframe remain
composition evidence rather than shipped interfaces. The older aperture plate
is fallback-only and the instrumentarium is retired from runtime. Further
portal work must generate or edit genuinely separable energy/core/route layers;
it must not repeatedly treat one flattened preview as the animation system.
