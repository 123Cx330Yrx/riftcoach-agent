# Portal Material Plate Generation Gate

## Purpose

The source-derived shift proof was rejected because moving a highlight while
leaving its original pixels in the mother image creates ghosting. This gate
defines what an independent material plate must provide before another local
composition or paid video call is considered.

## Required assets

1. **Rift interior plate**: transparent or cleanly keyable broad fluid depth,
   asymmetrical currents and sparse filaments; no hard concentric ring or new
   aperture boundary.
2. **Road/reflection plate**: surface-following cyan caustic and reflection
   response that follows the existing road perspective; no floating HUD line.
3. **Right-field plate**: layered star-dust, constellation shimmer and terrain
   caustic with independent near/far separation; not a handful of decorative
   stars.
4. **Occlusion/backplate masks**: the moving plate's original location is
   removed or painted back with matching architecture/material so no duplicate
   edge remains.
5. **Central crystal plate**: only internal facet/refraction variation and a
   low-amplitude local swell; crystal silhouette, tower and platform geometry
   remain owned by the base image.

## Source and tool policy

- The v2 mother image remains the immutable structural source.
- Image2/Photoshop may be used only for the concrete plate/backplate tasks
  above. They must not repaint the full master or invent a replacement crystal.
- A generated plate must be inspected on transparency and over the real source
  before it enters a loop. If the plate cannot be removed cleanly, reject it.
- No external video model call is allowed until this gate has at least one
  visually credible plate set and a no-cost local composite.

## Acceptance gates

- base pixels outside masks remain unchanged and sharp;
- no ghosting or softened architectural edges at 100% review;
- each of left/center/right and near/mid/far has a named material carrier;
- motion is clearly perceptible but not a global exposure pulse;
- 8-second periodic render has no duplicated/frozen end and passes encoded seam
  `DSSIM <= 0.03`;
- rejected plates remain outside runtime and production manifest.

## Current status

The `replace-shifted` visible proof used `motion_scale=2.5` and was rejected.
It is retained only as a negative example. The next action is to prepare the
plate/backplate asset specification and check Image2/Photoshop availability;
there is no automatic paid generation.
