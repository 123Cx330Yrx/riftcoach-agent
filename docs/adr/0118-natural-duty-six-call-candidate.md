# ADR0118: Six-call natural-duty candidate, publication closed

2026-10-14. Accepted for offline candidate engineering after the user's explicit
“确认” to the concrete six-call proposal. No paid batch or production admission.

## Decision and reason

Keep Agent-selected retrieval, two complete initial duties, deterministic exact
patching and two fresh duties. One tool-selection turn plus report generation
makes the shortest repaired path six model calls. The old five-call contract
cannot finish it. Adopt a separate candidate with max_calls=6; retain 401920
total tokens, 900 seconds for the whole task, 300 seconds per actual request,
64000 input/32768 output limits, zero retries, Flash/high generation and GLM/high
review. No Flash editing call is added. More retrieval rounds can still exhaust
the budget; six calls do not guarantee task completion.

Use contract 1.6.0 / natural-duty-direct-patch-time300-v1, Skill 0.7.0 and Program
4.0.0. The exact singleton, snapshot, source fingerprints and request receipts
bind this candidate separately from the development time600 identity and the
old five-call role contracts. Changes live in codex/natural-duty-six, based on
1e0e48af; the original backend checkout and frozen evidence stay intact.

## Semantic and publication boundary

Structural compatibility and exact application do not prove that an opinion
is correct, that two corrections agree semantically, or that correct content is
preserved semantically. Development Host reviews are not a product service.
Therefore the candidate always returns FAIL to the existing publication Harness,
including after two fresh passes. Its separate journal records
candidate_initial_pass_unqualified or candidate_repaired_pass_unqualified.
No Worker/API/default registration or ready-to-use paid transport is introduced.

This deliberately ends the authorized engineering package at a testable internal
composition. It is not the final product publication policy. Before admission,
evaluate reviewer false positives, bad corrections, semantic conflicts and actual
new reports under the same candidate. Do not turn offline fixtures or old familiar
successes into that evidence. Formal same-version 15 and natural consumption
remain dependencies; any paid proposal needs its own bounded authorization.

## Alternatives and consequences

Keeping five calls stops ordinary repair. Pre-fetching instead of Agent-selected
retrieval changes the task mechanism. Combining duties changes review isolation;
parallel execution changes latency, not the call count. The user selected six
calls for this candidate; these alternatives are not silently substituted.

Reuse the real application compiler, Runtime, shared budget, dispatch fence,
local RAG, Harness, Trace and receipt store. Use injected scripted providers for
offline checks. Reuse strict block/anchor/overlap validation and programmatic
patch assembly, with at least two remaining call slots before application.
The single 900-second clock and actual-plus-reserved-token gate still govern
every send. Known usage and unknown reservations retain their separate meanings.

Four existing runtime manifests are refreshed only for changed shared source
rows and their derived program hashes, inside the new worktree. This describes
the new source revision, not a re-signing or requalification of historical runs.
Default imports do not eagerly load the candidate's development dependencies.
